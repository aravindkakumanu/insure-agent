## Patterns worth a decision (not acted on)

1. **No "out of scope" behaviour.** Nothing tells the system to say "this policy does not
   address that". Out-of-scope questions get padded answers (G auto, market value) or an
   answer from general knowledge (tax). Confidence stays high because it measures grounding.
2. **The fixed IS-covered / NOT-covered layout forces filler** when one side is empty.
3. **Routing to Claims** for "what will I get back?" (burglary) and "someone may sue me"
   (icy driveway) gives process text; burglary's answer lost the jewelry limit.
4. **Confidence is high on wrong answers** (credit monitoring, auto deductible).
5. **Retrieval-tier misses can be rescued by the agents' own queries** (car), so the
   retrieval tier alone understates the system; the fence and the jewelry limit were
   not rescued.
